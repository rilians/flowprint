# MASTER BUILD PROMPT — FLOWPRINT
## Business Process Intelligence & Automation Opportunity Platform

You are acting as a Senior Full-Stack Engineer, Product Designer, UX Architect, and SaaS Product Developer.

Your task is to design and build a complete, production-quality web application named **FLOWPRINT**.

Do not build a generic dashboard, basic CRUD application, or static UI prototype.

Build a functional SaaS product that helps organizations visualize business processes, discover operational inefficiencies, identify automation opportunities, simulate process improvements, and generate professional business analysis reports.

The application must combine excellent visual design, meaningful business logic, interactive workflow visualization, accurate calculations, persistent data, and a coherent user experience.

---

# 1. PRODUCT IDENTITY

**Product:** FLOWPRINT

**Category:** Business Process Intelligence & Automation Opportunity Platform

**Tagline:** See the Work. Find the Waste. Design What's Next.

**Core Product Concept:**

FLOWPRINT enables users to:

1. Create visual representations of existing business processes.
2. Analyze operational workload and identify bottlenecks.
3. Discover activities that are suitable for automation or simplification.
4. Create improved versions of existing workflows.
5. Simulate the impact of proposed changes.
6. Generate professional process improvement reports.

FLOWPRINT is a process intelligence and process improvement design tool.

It is NOT primarily:
- A project management platform.
- A task management application.
- A generic workflow execution platform.
- A generic analytics dashboard.
- An AI chatbot.

The key differentiator is the complete journey from **As-Is Process → Process Intelligence → Improvement Opportunities → To-Be Scenario → Business Impact Report**.

The primary experience must communicate this journey visually.

# 12. AUTOMATION DISCOVERY ENGINE

Route: `/app/processes/[id]/opportunities`

Build a deterministic, rule-based analysis engine.

This is not an AI chatbot.

The recommendation engine must analyze activity properties and produce transparent improvement recommendations.

## Opportunity Scoring Formula

Total score: 0–100.

| Factor | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Monthly Volume | 20 |
| Manual Work Intensity | 20 |
| Repetitiveness | 20 |
| Standardization | 15 |
| Waiting Time | 10 |
| Error Impact | 10 |
| Digital Integration Readiness | 5 |

Use these formulas:

```ts
volumeScore = Math.min(monthlyVolume / 500, 1);

manualScore =
  workMode === "manual" ? 1 :
  workMode === "semi_automated" ? 0.5 : 0;

repeatabilityScore = (repeatability - 1) / 4;

standardizationScore = (standardization - 1) / 4;

waitingScore = Math.min(waitMinutes / 120, 1);

errorScore = (errorImpact - 1) / 4;

integrationScore = (integrationReadiness - 1) / 4;

opportunityScore =
  volumeScore * 20 +
  manualScore * 20 +
  repeatabilityScore * 20 +
  standardizationScore * 15 +
  waitingScore * 10 +
  errorScore * 10 +
  integrationScore * 5;
```

Validate and clamp all inputs before calculating.

Round displayed scores to the nearest whole number.

## Opportunity Classifications

- 75–100: High Priority.
- 55–74: Medium Priority.
- 0–54: Low Priority.

Label this score as an opportunity prioritization heuristic.

Do not present it as a probability of automation success.

## Recommendation Rules

Create rules for:

### Automate

Prioritize activities with:
- High monthly volume.
- Manual execution.
- High repeatability.
- High standardization.
- Digital integration readiness.

### Simplify

Flag a process when the user has identified unnecessary complexity, redundant steps, or excessive effort.

Do not assume a step is unnecessary just because it takes a long time.

### Standardize

Prioritize tasks with low standardization.

### Integrate

Identify tasks involving repeated transfers between digital systems.

### Reduce Waiting

Identify significant waiting periods and explain the likely operational effect.

## Opportunity Cards

Every recommendation card must include:

- Activity name.
- Score.
- Priority.
- Recommendation category.
- Explanation.
- Contributing factors.
- Recommended next action.
- Estimated improvement focus.
- Add to Scenario button.
- Dismiss button.

Provide filtering by category and priority.

Users must be able to inspect the reasoning behind each recommendation.

## Financial Calculations

```ts
baselineMonthlyHours =
  (baselineHumanEffortMinutes / 60) * monthlyVolume;

proposedMonthlyHours =
  (proposedHumanEffortMinutes / 60) * monthlyVolume;

monthlyHoursDifference =
  baselineMonthlyHours - proposedMonthlyHours;

potentialMonthlyHoursSaved =
  monthlyHoursDifference * adoptionRate;

potentialMonthlyCostSavings =
  potentialMonthlyHoursSaved * hourlyLaborCost;

paybackMonths =
  implementationCost / potentialMonthlyCostSavings;
```

Adoption rate must be represented as a decimal from 0 to 1 internally.

If savings are zero or negative:
- Do not display a misleading positive saving.
- Do not calculate a misleading payback period.
- Explain why the scenario does not currently generate positive estimated savings.

Always identify all results as estimates.
